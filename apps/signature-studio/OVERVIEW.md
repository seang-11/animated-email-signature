# Signature Studio

A branded email signature builder by Prospect Automations. People sign in, pick a template (or start blank), and design a professional email signature with a live preview, then copy it into their email app. Each person can keep multiple signatures — e.g. a full one for new emails and a compact one for replies.

**Who it's for:** Prospect Automations team members, clients, and anyone who copies the template from the Zite marketplace.

**Key capabilities:**
- Template gallery with six designed starting points
- Live preview inside a mock email window, with drag-and-drop arrangement across Left / Main / Bottom zones
- Content editing: name, title, contacts, social links, logo & banner, call-to-action button
- Design controls: colors, fonts, sizing, spacing, dividers and icon styles
- Multiple signatures per person with default, rename, duplicate and delete
- Autosave, undo/redo and revert-to-saved
- Export for Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail, with setup instructions
- Guided tour and inline tips for first-time users
- A privacy-safe marketplace demo that shows fictional sample people
